# Review the requested artifact

Read the actual deliverable, its version, the current user requirements and selected reference roles. State what could be watched/heard/read. A missing source limits comparison; it does not invalidate every observable finding.

| Artifact | Check against the approved target |
| --- | --- |
| Script | Concrete hook and payoff, changing information, unfamiliar-viewer comprehension, selected borrowing, accurate product claims, brand realization, readable text/speech and target audio timing |
| Keyframe | Composition, subject scale/position, action, identity, user-selected style/outfit, light/time, physical plausibility, reference-role leakage and unintended edits |
| Video prompt | Accepted event and end state, ordered references, documented capabilities, shot/group boundary, action speed, gaze/geography, exact copy assigned to an appropriate production layer |
| Generated video | Actual camera and subject motion, contact and continuity through cuts, identity/style, pacing, gaze, required events and defects throughout the clip |
| Final edit | Source in-points, action joins, music accents, dialogue/SFX, text/UI/brand layers, readable ending, duration, audio coverage and requested export |

Use the script skill's [advertising checks](../../mixreel-script/references/ad-script.md) when reviewing an advertising script. A functioning renderer or completed generation status cannot answer these creative questions.

For a complete script, verify actual shot IDs, scale/viewpoint, blocking, camera, end states and joins, plus a sound plan and early treatment of known generation limits. Do not accept a timed plot outline as a director-facing CG script. Check the audience desire/agency against the brief and the opening-to-payoff mechanism against reference evidence; a rejected emotional premise remains a failed creative check.

Read audience-facing copy without production notes. Flag internal review labels that accidentally became captions, while retaining disclosures needed to keep the product portrayal accurate. Check whether camera choices support the planned reveal or action rather than repeating a convenient default.

## Findings and closure

For each issue record the version and location, target, observed difference, impact, confidence, earliest likely cause, narrow repair destination and how to recheck. Separate evidence of a defect from a hypothesis about its cause. Prioritize failures that change story/product meaning or make the result unusable over cosmetic preferences.

Use pass, fail and not checked for each applicable dimension. An agent review is not a user acceptance. Record accepted preferences without converting them into universal aesthetic rules. Avoid unsupported aggregate quality scores.

Repair authorization follows the original task and budget. A review-only task produces findings; a repair task invokes the responsible stage and preserves unaffected assets. Do not introduce provider switches, new paid trials or story changes merely to close the review.

After repair inspect both the reported location and full context for regressions. Record evidence and outcome per finding. If the cause remains uncertain or the new result does not improve, revise the diagnosis before another paid attempt.
