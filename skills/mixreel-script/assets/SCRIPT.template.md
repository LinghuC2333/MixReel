# Script proposal

## Intent and evidence

Record artifact version, audience, purpose, selected message or creative goal, relevant product/story sources, confirmed constraints and requested stop point. Keep product claims and their conditions traceable. Mark fictional additions and unavailable evidence.

## Direction

Describe the recommended direction through actual opening/development/ending events. Include meaningful alternatives only when useful or requested, with the trade-off. Explain how the product or creative goal appears in the viewer's experience.

## Actual script

| Target time or pacing | Image, action and camera | Exact text or dialogue | Music and sound | Product/brand realization and transition |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

Keep source timestamps distinct from target times. Mark the target music excerpt, phrase/accents and timing confidence, or state that musical alignment is pending. Document intentional montage gaps.

## Self-review and next step

Record concrete revisions made after reading the sequence as an unfamiliar viewer. List remaining unknowns, accepted/proposed decisions, supplied assets, missing exact UI/copy and production constraints. State what can be reviewed now. Do not invent user approval, a production plan or a paid job.
