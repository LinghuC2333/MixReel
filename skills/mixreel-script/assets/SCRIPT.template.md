# Script proposal

## Intent and evidence

Record artifact version, audience, purpose, the one message or creative goal, the brand files used with their dates, relevant product or story sources, confirmed constraints and requested stop point. Keep product claims and their conditions traceable to `brand/brand-context.md`. Mark fictional additions and unavailable evidence.

## The idea

State the key message, the one event in a sentence, the longing it serves, the familiar things it uses with their evidence, how the product appears, and the rights position of anything that belongs to others, such as the music. Then describe the film as it plays. Include alternatives only when requested.

## Why it should work

Explain, against [marketing idea](../references/marketing-idea.md), what the viewer feels at each stretch and what holds them to the next, and how the product enters. Mark what comes from the user, audience evidence, a reference and the agent's judgment.

## Reference sheet

Attach labelled reference images when they exist, such as frames from the brand's taste library, official character, costume and background art, and product screenshots. Name the shot each image supports and its source.

## Early production feasibility

Record edit length, the shots made as video, image and post, the previs renderer and renders when one was built, known duration/input/audio limits and their evidence, provisional groups when needed, boundary states and sound continuity. Keep unknown request settings and prices unknown. Detailed verified execution belongs to video planning.

## Self-review and next step

Record concrete revisions made after reading the sequence as an unfamiliar viewer. List remaining unknowns, accepted/proposed decisions, supplied assets, missing exact UI/copy and production constraints. State what can be reviewed now. Do not invent user approval, a production plan or a paid job.
