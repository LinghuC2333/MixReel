# Script proposal

## Intent and evidence

Record artifact version, audience, purpose, the one message or creative goal, the brand files used with their dates, relevant product or story sources, confirmed constraints and requested stop point. Keep product claims and their conditions traceable to `brand/brand-context.md`. Mark fictional additions and unavailable evidence.

## The idea

State the desire this film serves, the scene and characters that deliver it, the familiar point with its evidence and what is new, and the rights position of anything that belongs to others. Then describe the film through its actual opening, escalation and payoff. Include alternatives only when useful or requested, with the trade-off.

## Why it should work

State the viewer's viewpoint, the first-frame stimulus, the expectation it opens, how each beat raises it, where the viewer's choice sits and why they would want it, and the payoff. Explain who decides and how the product enters the story. Mark what comes from the user, audience evidence, a reference and the agent's judgment.

## Reference sheet

Attach labelled reference images when they exist, such as frames from the brand's taste library, official character, costume and background art, and product screenshots. Name the shot each image supports and its source.

## Early production feasibility

Record edit length, generated versus post-production footage, known duration/input/audio limits and their evidence, provisional groups when needed, boundary states and sound continuity. Keep unknown request settings and prices unknown. Detailed verified execution belongs to video planning.

## Self-review and next step

Record concrete revisions made after reading the sequence as an unfamiliar viewer. List remaining unknowns, accepted/proposed decisions, supplied assets, missing exact UI/copy and production constraints. State what can be reviewed now. Do not invent user approval, a production plan or a paid job.
