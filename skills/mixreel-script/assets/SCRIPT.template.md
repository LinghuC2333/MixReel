# Script proposal

## Intent and evidence

Record artifact version, audience, purpose, selected message or creative goal, relevant product/story sources, confirmed constraints and requested stop point. Keep product claims and their conditions traceable. Mark fictional additions and unavailable evidence.

## Borrowing decisions

| Dimension and source time | Observed mechanism | Keep, adapt, exclude or pending | Target treatment and reason | Decision authority |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

## Direction

Describe the recommended direction through actual opening/development/ending events. Include meaningful alternatives only when useful or requested, with the trade-off. Explain how the product or creative goal appears in the viewer's experience.

## Audience appeal

State viewpoint, brief-supported desire, exact opening stimulus, expectation and payoff, with reference evidence and creative uncertainty. Explain who decides and how the product changes the visible outcome.

## Actual script

| Shot ID and target time | Scale, angle, placement, camera and causal action | End state and next-shot connection | Exact copy/dialogue | Sound cue and product/edit-layer realization |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

Keep source timestamps distinct from target times. Mark the target music excerpt, phrase/accents and timing confidence, or give the proposed original/sound-only treatment and mark exact alignment provisional. Document intentional montage gaps.

## Early production feasibility

Record edit length, generated versus post-production footage, known duration/input/audio limits and their evidence, provisional groups when needed, boundary states and sound continuity. Keep unknown request settings and prices unknown. Detailed verified execution belongs to video planning.

## Self-review and next step

Record concrete revisions made after reading the sequence as an unfamiliar viewer. List remaining unknowns, accepted/proposed decisions, supplied assets, missing exact UI/copy and production constraints. State what can be reviewed now. Do not invent user approval, a production plan or a paid job.
