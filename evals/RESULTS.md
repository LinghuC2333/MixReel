## Script results and correction

The synthetic product trial used only manually moving a task from Today to Tomorrow. Its output borrowed a pause and delayed reveal through reframing, then showed pressing, dragging and releasing the card. It did not invent automated scheduling or a free offer, or require a novel or provider account. The [full synthetic output](outputs/dayboard-script.md) is included so reviewers can judge the actual words and sequence. The product and scenario are test fixtures.

The private campaign trial used the advertiser's supplied document and actual reference frames. It compared two concrete scenarios and developed one with product behavior, exact copy and provisional timing. Private product details and source files remain outside this repository.

That trial also exposed a writing problem. Internal evidence and review annotations became audience captions, and camera instructions repeated fixed setups without explaining their visual purpose. The script and review references were amended to separate review-only annotations from finished copy, retain necessary product disclosures, and choose camera behavior for the reveal or action. Neither every-shot motion nor removing all disclosures is required.

The same independent campaign trial was rerun against the amended rules, preserving its first draft. The revision removed internal review text from audience captions, retained conditional product language, and gave one reveal a motivated lateral move while explaining why other shots stayed still. This fixes the observed writing issues; no generated footage or human creative approval was claimed.

The changed-music walkthrough identified a source-lyric dependency and proposed replacing the reveal mechanism instead of stretching every timestamp. This was an author-led fixture, not an audio-listening result.
